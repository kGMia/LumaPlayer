Upstream files under shaders/ are unmodified. The plugin generates variants in
its own data folder: DESC labels identify executed passes; CAS WHEN permits
pre-scaling, transfer macros match the reported SDR gamma, and SHARPENING uses
the slider. Modified runtime variants are generated by src/main.ts (2026-10-12).
GPL and LGPL texts are included in licenses/. All shaders remain replaceable.
